# Game Concepts — the Source of Truth

These are the ten concepts agreed at the start of the project. **Build every new game from this document**, not from memory or a summary: an earlier summary drifted (it gave Shine Crew pigeons to shoo away, which breaks the core rule below).

## The rules every game follows
- Each game has its own action verb. Only two auto-fire (Burj Khalifa, Fountain), and there you clean or conduct, never attack.
- **No animal is ever hit, chased off or annoyed.** Animals are scenery, rewards or friends.
- **Every landmark ends each run looking better than it started.** Nothing may upset the location's owners.
- One finger. Endless waves, a boss every 5 waves, short power-ups, fun for ages 3–10 *and* adults.
- Blocky art, but original: no Minecraft name, textures or characters.

## The original concept list (verbatim)

Ten games, each with a different action verb. Only two use auto-firing (Burj Khalifa and the Fountain), and in both you're cleaning or conducting, never attacking. No animal is ever hit, chased off or annoyed, and every landmark ends each run looking better than it started. The whole world is built from blocks, and the blocks drive the gameplay too: you build the Frame block by block, replace hull blocks on the space station, and blast sand-crust blocks off the Burj.

**1. Dolphinarium: "Juggle Show" (juggle)**
- **The real spot:** The dolphins and seals are trained to perform tricks and stunts, including singing and balancing balls. In the bird show, a cockatoo paints, a macaw raises a flag and plays basketball, and an African grey parrot talks.
- **How it plays:** You're the fur seal. Drag left and right, and balls bounce off your nose automatically. Trainers and dolphins keep lobbing in more balls, and any ball that hits the water costs a life.
- **Boss:** The macaw's basketball round. Heavy balls drop from the rafters while the parrot cheers you on in real time.
- **Adult hook and boosts:** Keeping 7 balls up at once takes real skill, and balancing one on your nose builds a multiplier while the others fall. Boosts are a trainer's whistle for slow-motion, a fish bucket for an extra life, and a dolphin that tail-flicks one ball back up for you.

**2. Butterfly Garden: "Fruit Rush" (chop)**
- **The real spot:** Fifteen feeding stations serve the butterflies nutrient syrups and fruit like mango, pineapple and watermelon, across ten climate-controlled domes kept at 24°C, with a koi pond in Dome 2.
- **How it plays:** Keepers toss fruit into the air and you swipe to slice it. The slices land on the trays, and bigger combos attract rarer species. The butterflies are only ever the reward, never an obstacle. The hazards are syrup bottles, which make your blade sticky for 3 seconds, and coconuts, which bounce your blade and break your combo. Any fruit you miss drops into the koi pond, costing you a life.
- **Boss:** A giant watermelon that needs 10 fast slices before it lands.
- **Adult hook and boosts:** It's Fruit Ninja with a collection layer: 50 species to unlock, from common visitors up to the Monarch, Tailed Jay and Great Eggfly. Boosts are slow-motion, a wider blade, and a fruit fountain that triples the fruit for 5 seconds.

**3. Burj Khalifa: "Shine Crew" (auto-fire)**
- **The real spot:** A team of 36 workers takes three to four months to clean all 24,348 windows. The cleaning machines park inside the building and travel out on customised tracks.
- **How it plays:** You drag the cleaning cradle along the facade while a pressure washer sprays automatically at the nearest sand crust. The thick crusts have a core block you have to hit to break them. The wind swings the cradle like a pendulum.
- **Boss:** A sandstorm wall that coats a whole section at once, and you have to clear it before it passes.
- **Adult hook and boosts:** It plays like Archero-style positioning with vertigo physics at 800 m, and a lifetime counter tracks your progress toward all 24,348 windows. Boosts are a wide nozzle, a rain shower that cleans the whole screen, and a second crew cradle for 10 seconds.

**4. Dubai Frame: "Frame Builder" (place)**
- **The real spot:** The Frame's 150 m height and 93 m width follow the golden ratio, it is clad in gold-toned steel as a nod to Dubai's old name, the City of Gold, and a 93 m glass-floored walkway joins the two towers. The glass floor suddenly turns clear halfway across the bridge.
- **How it plays:** You drag the crane hook and blocks drop on a steady beat, alternating between the left and right towers. You have to keep both towers level.
- **Boss:** At 150 m, you lower the bridge across both towers in the wind. If the towers are uneven, the bridge won't seat. A perfect placement turns the glass floor clear.
- **Adult hook and boosts:** It's Stack with a two-tower balancing problem. The view changes as you climb, with the Creek and souks to the north and the Burj Khalifa and Business Bay to the south. Boosts are a laser guide, a slow crane, and a gold block that auto-levels the towers.

**5. Dubai Fountain: "Fountain Conductor" (auto-fire)**
- **The real spot:** The fountain uses Shooters, Oarsmen and water robots. The Extreme Shooters reach 500 ft but fire least often, because they need time to build pressure, and every Shooter makes a loud boom. The fountain is laid out as five circles and two arcs, and a traditional abra takes visitors across Burj Lake.
- **How it plays:** You move the nozzle rig around the circles and arcs, and the jets fire automatically on every beat. You have to be under each light cue when its beat lands. Abras cruise through the lake, and soaking one is a penalty.
- **Boss:** The Extreme Shooter finale. You hold inside a ring for 3 beats to build pressure, then it booms.
- **Adult hook and boosts:** It's a real rhythm game in the Guitar Hero lineage, where each song is a wave. Boosts are Superlights for double score, an extra nozzle, and a slowed beat. Use original music only.

**6. Museum of the Future: "OSS Hope" (fix)**
- **The real spot:** Visitors blast off in a Falcon Space Capsule to the OSS Hope space station, set in 2071. In the Tomorrow Today exhibition, flying robots float and glide overhead. The station sits 600 km above Earth and explores how the moon could become a source of renewable energy.
- **How it plays:** You drift in zero gravity, so you steer with momentum. Micrometeoroids knock hull blocks loose, and drifting into a gap snaps a replacement block into place. If too many gaps stay open at once, the station goes dark.
- **Boss:** The moon-energy array overloads, and you fix 5 relay nodes while dodging its sweeping beam.
- **Adult hook and boosts:** Momentum physics is hard to master, and routing between gaps plays like a speedrun. Boosts are a robot helper, a jet burst, and a magnet that pulls loose blocks to you.

**7. Skydive Dubai: "Camera Flyer" (capture)**
- **The real spot:** Jumpers freefall for 60 seconds at 120 mph from 13,000 ft, and every jump is photographed and filmed. Below are the Palm Jumeirah, the Burj Al Arab and The World Islands.
- **How it plays:** You're the videographer, and each wave is one freefall. You hold each tandem pair in frame until the shutter fills.
- **Boss:** An 8-person formation you must frame before it breaks apart. Every jump ends with a canopy landing on a target.
- **Adult hook and boosts:** Shots are scored on composition, with bonus points for landmarks in the background, and your best frames save as shareable photos. Boosts are a wide-angle lens, burst mode, and a speed suit.

**8. Ski Dubai: "Penguin March" (herd)**
- **The real spot:** The park is home to Gentoo and King penguins, which parade around the park in the March of the Penguins. It also has zorbing, a bobsled that reaches 45 km/h, and a giant ice cave.
- **How it plays:** You lead the march and walk into stray penguins to grow the line behind you, snake-style. If a zorb, a tuber, or your own line cuts across the parade, the penguins behind the cut simply waddle back to the colony.
- **Boss:** A snow-gun blizzard hits while zorbs roll down the slope.
- **Adult hook and boosts:** It has slither.io tension: a longer line means a bigger score and harder steering. Boosts are hot cocoa from the Avalanche Cafe for speed, a fish bucket that pulls penguins to you like a magnet, and the chairlift to jump across the park.

**9. Desert: "Falcon Strike" (catch)**
- **The real spot:** Desert camps offer a falcon experience, since the falcon is a favourite animal of the Gulf. The Dubai Desert Conservation Reserve, the UAE's first national park, protects native animals like Arabian oryx and gazelles.
- **How it plays:** You're the falcon, and the falconer swings a lure in loops over the dunes. You drag to intercept it, and every catch makes the next arc faster.
- **Boss:** A sandstorm round where the lure only shows in flashes.
- **Adult hook and boosts:** Diving from higher up multiplies your speed and score but risks overshooting, and rival falcons from the same team compete for the lure. Boosts are a thermal updraft, falcon-eye slow-motion, and a tailwind. The oryx appear as scenery only.

**10. Safari Park: "Cheetah Run" (run and jump)**
- **The real spot:** The cheetah is the Explorer Village's hero animal, and families love watching the cheetahs in action in the cheetah-run area. A white rhino calf named Salam was born on March 1, 2026, and a giraffe calf named Zuri is thriving.
- **How it plays:** The cheetah runs automatically across the savanna. You drag to switch lanes, jump streams and logs, and weave through the herds. Brushing past an animal only makes you stumble and lose speed, and no animal is ever hit.
- **Boss:** A race against the Explorer Safari bus to the gate. Salam and Zuri appear along the route, and passing them gently earns a bonus.
- **Adult hook and boosts:** It has Temple Run speed with bonuses for perfect racing lines. Boosts are a 3-second sprint burst, a pounce for long jumps, and a second wind.

**Why adults will play and talk about these:**
- **One-hit hard mode.** It unlocks after the first boss and is where the bragging rights come from.
- **Leaderboards against real people.** A Dubai-wide leaderboard plus friends' ghost runs gives players someone specific to beat.
- **Auto-clipped best runs.** An 8-second replay of each best run is ready to share on WhatsApp or Instagram.

Two notes: I benched Aquaventure's "Tower Hop" if you want to swap it in. Keep the art blocky but original, with no Minecraft name, textures or characters.
